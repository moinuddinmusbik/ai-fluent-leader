## Steal This For Your Org

1. **60% audit:** List top 10 recurring tasks per high-volume role. 6+ tasks AI can do today = displacement zone. Plan upskilling now.
2. **"Which side?" exercise:** Map who directs AI vs. who does replaceable work per department. Where column 2 dominates = investment priority.
3. **Watch Examples 1–3 (3:34–7:44) with operations lead.** Map each to a comparable org function. Surfaces risk before it's a layoff conversation.
4. **Assign one "AI manager in training" per team this quarter.** Process-aware person + weeks of structured learning = faster than external hire.

## Mistake to Avoid

Treating displacement as future. The 60% claim and "hundreds of thousands already" framing make it present-tense. Scheduling "AI strategy for next quarter" while this quarter's workforce shrinks is the named mistake. *(Community corroboration: @abdar_brightwaveai, 3 likes: "We just had layoffs because of people automating their jobs... I can see myself getting to the point of getting let go because I've automated everything.")*
