# Gartner

**Type:** Research and Advisory Firm
**Headquarters:** Stamford, Connecticut, USA
**Relevance:** Premier technology analyst firm; one of the most-cited sources for enterprise AI market sizing, forecasts, and strategic predictions.

## Description

Gartner is a global research and advisory company that provides technology and business intelligence. For AI-fluent leaders, Gartner forecasts are critical inputs for board-level AI budget conversations, vendor selection, and strategic timing.

## Key AI-Relevant Contributions (2026)

- **July 20, 2026:** Forecast that worldwide AI platforms and models market will reach $64 billion in 2026 (+63.4% YoY); GenAI model spend alone up 117%.
- Prediction that 40% of enterprise applications will feature task-specific AI agents by end of 2026, up from <5% in 2025.
- Warning: CIOs have 3–6 months to define their AI agent strategies or risk competitive displacement.
- Ongoing emphasis on shifting competition from model capability to orchestration, governance, and ROI clarity.
