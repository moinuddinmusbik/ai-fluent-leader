# Booz Allen Hamilton

**Type:** Management Consulting and Technology Services Firm
**Relevance:** One of the largest US government IT contractors; their federal AI surveys provide a uniquely high-signal benchmark for enterprise AI deployment risk because government clients have the most rigorous security requirements.

## Description

Booz Allen Hamilton is a US-based management consulting firm specializing in management, technology, and cybersecurity services, predominantly for the US government and defense sector. Their AI research is a leading indicator of enterprise AI governance challenges.

## Key AI-Relevant Contributions (2026)

- **July 21, 2026:** Released survey of 105 federal IT/cybersecurity decision-makers finding that 58% of agencies are deploying or piloting AI agents, yet only 28% are highly confident in secure deployment.
- Survey findings: 79% very/extremely concerned about AI-accelerated cyberattacks in the next 12–18 months; 22% lack clear accountability structures for AI deployments.
- Trust-building levers identified: agent behavior visibility (56%), proven risk frameworks (44%), demonstrated success in own environments (42%).
- Published in Velocity, Booz Allen's emerging technology journal.
