# Domino Data Lab

**Type:** Enterprise AI/MLOps Platform Company
**Relevance:** Publishes a widely-cited annual Enterprise AI Report tracking production capability and ROI across large enterprises; one of the most longitudinal enterprise AI benchmarks available.

## Description

Domino Data Lab provides an enterprise MLOps and data science platform. Their Fifth Annual Enterprise AI Report (2026) surveyed 639 senior enterprise AI leaders across North America, UK, and continental Europe in financial services, life sciences, and public sector.

## Key AI-Relevant Contributions (2026)

- **July 22, 2026:** Fifth Annual Enterprise AI Report finds that 93% of enterprise AI leaders report improved production capability in 2026 (up from 88% in 2025), yet 57% still fail to generate ROI exceeding investment — a number unchanged from 2025.
- Documents the governance-ROI correlation: European organizations with lowest integrated governance (42.6%) also underperform on ROI vs. North America (~51% integrated governance).
- Identifies top-2 organizational priorities: expanding agentic AI (38.5%) and upskilling business users (38.5%) — tied.
- Only 43% of agentic AI deployments are running with governance in place.
