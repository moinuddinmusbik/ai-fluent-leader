# Arctera

**Type:** Data Management and Compliance Technology Company (formerly part of Veritas)
**Relevance:** Provides AI governance and communications risk tools; their annual State of AI Governance report is a key compliance benchmark.

## Description

Arctera (spun out of Veritas in 2024) specializes in data management, compliance, and AI governance software. Their 2026 State of AI Governance report surveyed 500 compliance decision-makers across finance, healthcare, and energy/utilities in the Americas and EMEA.

## Key AI-Relevant Contributions (2026)

- **July 21, 2026:** Published "State of AI Governance 2026" finding that 78% of organizations expect communications risk to increase over the next 12–24 months, but only 19% have logging/retention/detection controls to prove governance readiness.
- Coined the distinction between AI policy (what AI should do) and AI auditability (proof of what it did) — only 19% have the latter.
- Survey conducted by Hanover Research in May 2026; 500 compliance professionals across finance, healthcare, and energy/utilities.
- Highlights the policy–auditability gap as the primary enterprise AI governance risk in regulated industries.
