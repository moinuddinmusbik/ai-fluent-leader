# Voice Discovery Skill

Introduced by [[nate-b-jones]] in the episode "AI Slop Is Costing You Hours. Here's How To Stop Sending It." (2026-08-05).

## Definition

A structured process for identifying and capturing your authentic writing voice as a reference artifact *before* using AI to draft or edit. The artifact is then used to steer the model's output toward your perspective rather than toward the [[hill-climbing-convergence]] mean.

## Why It Works

[[hill-climbing-convergence]] means models always converge on the same polished-but-generic output. Changing the prompt alone changes the path but not the destination. The Voice Discovery Skill changes the starting point: by establishing a concrete, personal reference before prompting, it shifts where the model begins its climb — producing output that amplifies your perspective rather than erasing it.

## What a Style Guide Cannot Do

A style guide prescribes rules about outputs (vocabulary, sentence structure, tone). The Voice Discovery Skill establishes a reference about the *mind behind the work*: what you mean, why you care, how you argue. Style rules are applied after prompting; voice is established before.

## Role in [[pro-authorship]]

The Voice Discovery Skill is the operational component of [[pro-authorship]] — the tool that makes "be your own author" a repeatable process rather than a vague aspiration.
