# The Agency Trap

When a cheaper AI model enters the market — via open weights, a new provider, or a pricing war — the cost reduction flows to all competitors equally. The trap: if your AI advantage lives entirely at the model tier (which API you call), the edge disappears as soon as the model commoditizes.

The differentiator that survives commoditization is what you have *built on top* of the model that a rival cannot buy from the same API: proprietary data, tuned workflows, internal integrations, institutional knowledge embedded in prompts and fine-tunes.

**Origin:** Named and explained by [[nate-b-jones]] in the context of Kimi K3's open-weight release (2026-08-04). The parallel: Kimi K3 drives down API pricing for every company simultaneously — if your advantage was the price you paid for OpenAI/Anthropic, that advantage is now gone for you and your competitors equally.
