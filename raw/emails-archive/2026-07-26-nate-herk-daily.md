## Steal This For Your Org

1. **Run a "60% audit" on your highest-volume roles.** List top 10 recurring tasks per role. Mark what AI agents can do today. 6+ marks = displacement zone — plan upskilling now.
2. **Run Nate's "which side?" exercise with your leadership team.** Map who is directing AI vs. who is doing work AI will replace. Prioritize upskilling investment where the second column dominates.
3. **Watch the three job examples (3:34–7:44) with your operations lead.** Map each to a comparable function in your org — surfaces specific displacement risk before it's a layoff conversation.
4. **Assign one "AI manager in training" per team this quarter.** Weeks to competency, no technical background required — pick the most process-aware person on each team.

## Mistake to Avoid

Treating agentic AI displacement as a future problem. Nate's claim — 60% of tasks, hundreds of thousands already displaced — frames this as ongoing. The most common mistake: scheduling an "AI strategy review" for next quarter while the workforce on the other side of the fork shrinks this quarter. *(Real-world corroboration: @abdar_brightwaveai, 3 likes: "We just had layoffs because of people automating their jobs...
